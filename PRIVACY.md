# Privacy

- Without signing in, everything you do stays in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- If you sign in, we store your **email address** (for the sign-in code) and **your saved work** (cells, panels and PLC projects) in a Supabase database. Nothing else is collected: no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- Only you can read your saved work. The project owner can see email addresses in the Supabase dashboard to run the service.
- To delete your account and your saved work, open an issue or email the project owner; it will be removed within 30 days.
- Sign-in emails are sent by Supabase (or the email service configured for it).
