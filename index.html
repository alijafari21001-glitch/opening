Learn PLCs, HMIs, robots and panel wiring on one live production line.

A free, open source trainer that runs in your browser. The PLC, the HMI, two robots and three electrical cabinets share the same live I/O, so a broken wire stops a real robot.

What students practice

  • Writing and debugging ladder logic online
  • Building HMI screens and alarm lists
  • Teaching robots with a teach pendant, frames and position registers
  • Exporting programs as .LS files for a real controller
  • Reading drawings, wire numbers and I/O lists
  • Finding hidden faults with a multimeter
  • Lockout thinking: E-stops, fence gates, servo power
  • Order settings, cycle time and line flow

For instructors
